Cheran Groups ERP — Relational Schema & State Machine

Strict Transactional Integrity

Multi-stage inventory mutations managed with PostgreSQL SERIALIZABLE transactions and row locking, preventing double-allocation and stock discrepancy during peak production shifts.

Work-in-Progress (WIP) State Machine

Formal state transitions for shop-floor job cards, tracking raw materials consumption through intermediate processing to finished goods dispatch.

Query & Index Optimization

Composite indexing on composite entity keys and materialized views for operational rollups, reducing complex inventory aggregation queries from 1.8s to under 45ms.

Audit Trails & Observability

Immutable event log tables capturing every ledger adjustment and operator authorization for end-to-end statutory compliance.
